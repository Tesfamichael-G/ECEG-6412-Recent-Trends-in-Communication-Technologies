# Solutions — ECEG-6412

Key steps and final answers for the tutorial problems at the end of each
chapter. The **worked examples** in the chapters contain full derivations;
these pages give the essential steps and results so you can check your work.

!!! tip "Use sparingly"
    Attempt the problem first, then consult the solution.

---

## How each solution page is organised

Every page mirrors its chapter:

- **One section per problem** — same numbering as in the chapter.
- **Key steps** in 2–4 lines — enough to follow the argument.
- **Final answer** in bold.

If a step is unclear, follow the **"See Example N.M"** reference back to the
chapter.
