# ECEG-6412 — Recent Trends in Communication Technologies

**MSc in Communication Engineering · Year 1, Semester I · Elective I**

This course surveys the wireless propagation channel, modulation and multiple-access
techniques, and the major generations of mobile systems: from AMPS/TACS through GSM
and UMTS to LTE, WiMAX, 5G, and the emerging application of **machine learning** to
physical- and MAC-layer problems.

## What you will learn

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-broadcast: **Understand the wireless channel**

    ---

    Path loss, shadowing, multipath, Doppler, and the fading models (Rayleigh,
    Rician, Nakagami) that shape every wireless link.

- :material-signal: **Apply modulation, coding, and multiple access**

    ---

    BPSK/QPSK/QAM/OFDM, TDMA/FDMA/CDMA/SDMA, error-control and source coding —
    the toolbox behind every cellular standard.

- :material-cellphone: **Analyze 2G–5G cellular systems**

    ---

    AMPS/TACS → GSM → UMTS → LTE → 5G NR: architecture, physical layer, protocols,
    and evolution.

- :material-robot: **Explore emerging trends**

    ---

    Wired technology trends, NGN (Bluetooth, ZigBee, WiFi, UWB), IoT, and the
    emerging role of machine learning in the PHY and MAC layers.

</div>

## Chapters

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-numeric-1-circle: **Introduction**

    ---

    History of wireless, standards landscape, layered cellular view, wireless
    and cellular environments, propagation channel, legacy AMPS/TACS.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 1](chapters/01-introduction.md)

- :material-numeric-2-circle: **Modulation and Coding for Wireless Systems**

    ---

    Analogue and digital modulation (BPSK, QPSK, QAM, OFDM), multiple access
    (TDMA, FDMA, CDMA, SDMA), error control and source coding.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 2](chapters/02-modulation-coding.md)

- :material-ethernet: **Wired Technology Trends**

    ---

    Access loop, core network, and distribution networks — the wired backbone
    that mobile systems depend on.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Wired Trends](chapters/wired-trends.md)

- :material-numeric-3-circle: **GSM**

    ---

    System architecture, physical layer, channels, data and services,
    2.5G evolution (HSCSD, GPRS, EDGE, EGPRS).

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 3](chapters/03-gsm.md)

- :material-numeric-4-circle: **UMTS**

    ---

    Migration from GSM, architecture, physical layer, coding, network design,
    and 3.5G/3.75G (HSDPA, HSUPA, HSPA).

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 4](chapters/04-umts.md)

- :material-numeric-5-circle: **Advanced Broadband Wireless**

    ---

    4G technologies, LTE, WiMAX, network architecture, capacity, radio components.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 5](chapters/05-broadband-wireless.md)

- :material-numeric-6-circle: **Next Generation Networks**

    ---

    NGN overview, PANs (Bluetooth, ZigBee), WiFi, UWB, 5G and IoT.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 6](chapters/06-ngn.md)

- :material-numeric-7-circle: **Machine Learning for Communications**

    ---

    The emerging-technology initiative: ML for the PHY and MAC layers across
    wireless, optical, satellite, and molecular communication.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Chapter 7](chapters/07-ml-communications.md)

</div>
